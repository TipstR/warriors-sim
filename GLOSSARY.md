# Warriors Sim

The words used for the simulated Clan life, in the code, the logs and the docs.

## Cats

**Queen**:
A she-cat who is expecting kits or nursing them.
_Avoid_: Mother (a mother whose kits are weaned is no longer a queen)

**Queen in the nursery**:
A queen who stays with the kits: nursing, or close to giving birth. A queen early in her pregnancy still works.

**Confined**:
Too sick or injured to leave the nest.
_Avoid_: Bedridden

## Chores

**Chore**:
A small task in camp that some cat has to do, waiting until a cat responsible for it takes it.
_Avoid_: Job, duty, blackboard entry

**The Clan's chores**:
All chores of a Clan that are open right now. In programming terms the "blackboard".
_Avoid_: Task list, job board

**Small chore**:
A chore done in passing, next to the cat's other work in the same tick.

**Big chore**:
A chore that takes the cat's whole tick.

**Bringing prey**:
The chore of carrying a cat's piece from the fresh-kill pile to a cat who stays in its den: a queen in the nursery, an elder, or a sick cat. The eating order decides the piece. The chore only decides who carries it.
